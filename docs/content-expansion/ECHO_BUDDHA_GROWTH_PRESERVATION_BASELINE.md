## Source-literacy research baseline

Current authoritative reading material confirms that beginners need orientation beyond a definition of “sutta”:

- SuttaCentral introduction and canon/navigation context: https://suttacentral.net/introduction
- SuttaCentral Getting Started reading approaches: https://suttacentral.net/start
- Bhikkhu Sujato's reader guide, including reference conventions and translation context: https://suttacentral.net/edition/dn/en/sujato/general_introduction
- Access to Insight's “Befriending the Suttas” reading guide: https://www.accesstoinsight.org/befriending.html

Current search results contain beginner reading guides, but Echo Buddha's opportunity is narrower and better governed: a source-aware method for identifying the text, understanding the reference, separating translation from summary, noticing setting and repetition, comparing a key passage, and carrying one practice question into daily life.

## Baseline risk decision

The approved Wave 2 scope is one P1 source-literacy page: `/learn/sutta-for-daily-life/how-to-read-a-buddhist-sutta/`. It does not redefine “sutta,” replace the Pali Canon dictionary page, summarize a new discourse, or compete with a current winner. Two low-risk inbound integrations are approved: automatic inclusion on the Sutta for Daily Life hub and one curated link from Buddhist Resources.

All other tested ideas are retained as P2/P3 or rejected. No existing winner improvement case passed the benefit-versus-regression gate.
