# Search-intent and ownership review

## Q10

Owner: relic miracle narratives and whether a reported wonder authenticates a claimed Buddha relic. Q2 continues to own the broader meaning of post-Parinibbāna respect; Q9 continues to own comparative cetiya merit. Q10 earns an independent route by joining DN 16's funeral narrative, later `Mahāvaṃsa` traditions, and a careful provenance boundary.

## Q11

Owner: whether absence of physical acceptance or use invalidates an offering's merit. It does not replace Q2's broader devotional overview or Q9's comparison question. DN 16, Vimānavatthu 47, AN 6.63, and MN 142 answer a distinct practical concern.

## Q12

Owner: why intending a particular donation is related to, but not identical with, completing it. The general karma pages remain the owners of introductory kamma definitions and everyday application. Q12 is narrower: both halves of AN 6.63, mental action, actual relinquishment, and the limited status of a later scholastic movement model.

Each page would remain independently useful without search engines or advertising. Titles, descriptions, related links, and search terms reflect the distinct owners rather than trivial query variants.
