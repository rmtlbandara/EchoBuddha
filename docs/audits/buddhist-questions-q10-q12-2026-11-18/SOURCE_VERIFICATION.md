# Source verification

Verified on 2026-11-18 against the linked public sources.

- DN 16 supports the cremation/relic account, divine participation and extraordinary funeral events, relic distribution and monuments, and later offerings/bowing/confidence at a Tathāgata monument. It does not state a miracle-authentication rule.
- `Mahāvaṃsa` chapter 17 describes the collarbone relic rising and displaying the twin miracle, linked to the Buddha's prior resolutions. Chapter 31 contains a separate rising/Buddha-form/twin-miracle enshrinement narrative. Both are labeled later Theravāda chronicle.
- John Strong's academic study supplies broad historical context for relic veneration, not a judgment on metaphysical possibility.
- AN 6.63 supplies both propositions used: intention is kamma; intending, one acts through body, speech, or mind.
- Vimānavatthu 47 contains the living/fully-quenched, same-mind/same-fruit devotional formulation. It is labeled a Theravāda canonical devotional verse, not an early prose discourse or universal merit formula.
- MN 142 identifies Mahāpajāpatī Gotamī as the Buddha's maternal aunt and nurse/foster mother, records the cloth gift's redirection to the Saṅgha, and says both Buddha and Saṅgha are honored. The page does not place a later complete motive into the Buddha's speech.
- AN 6.37 concerns an actual gift and donor/recipient factors before, during, and after giving.
- Visuddhimagga XIV.61 relates bodily intimation to consciousness-originated air/motion and displaying intention. It is presented only as a later Theravāda scholastic/commentarial model, not modern physiology.

No long modern translation is reproduced. Public prose is original synthesis with explicit source boundaries.
