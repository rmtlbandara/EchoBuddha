The Angriest Text You'll Never Send

Anger can write a message faster than we can understand what hurts. Before pressing Send, notice the heat in your body, feel your feet, and take one unhurried breath. Then ask: what actually needs to change?

Buddhist-inspired mindfulness and Right Speech invite honest words with care. You can tell the truth and set a boundary without letting the first angry draft choose your words.
