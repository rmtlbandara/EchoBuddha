# Echo Buddha Phase 1 — Executive Report

## Confirmed problems

- Six priority clusters contained support pages with materially overlapping SERP promises.
- Thirteen article titles/H1s used repeated broad modifiers even where the body had a narrower useful job.
- Article templates emitted FAQPage markup that has no regular Google rich-result use for this publisher.
- Mixed explicitly numbered headings could receive a second visual list marker in the article TOC.
- Meditation detail pages displayed shared safety guidance and a second generic safety section.
- Intent governance did not cover every current indexable URL in one machine-readable registry.

- Related terminology was not treated as proof of cannibalization.
- Healthy mindfulness, non-attachment, Right Speech, handbook, quote, and Buddhist Questions architectures were retained.
- No evidence justified a redirect, deletion, noindex, cross-page canonical, or slug change.
