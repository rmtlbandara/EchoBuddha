The Handbook keeps source layers visible. Its early-discourse material is separated from later manuals, the Milindapañha, commentarial categories, traditional cosmology, and Sri Lankan regional practice. The Milindapañha is identified as a later Pāli work whose canonical status varies by regional recension. The Questions series distinguishes early memorial evidence, Ja 479 narrative/commentary, Apadāna hagiography, later image commentary, pilgrimage tradition, and art history. The audit checked the integrity of those distinctions. It did not independently date every image or commentarial sentence.
