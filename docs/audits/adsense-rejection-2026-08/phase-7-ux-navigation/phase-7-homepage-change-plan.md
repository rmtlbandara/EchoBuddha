# Phase 7 Homepage Change Plan

## Current section order

1. Hero with four actions and values
2. Five need paths, six guide cards, five resource links, repeated Start Here callout
3. Five-step beginner path plus daily-reflection card
4. Featured quote explanation/card
5. Ad container (disabled manual slot behavior)
6. Six latest articles
7. Eight featured quotes
8. Meditation starter
9. Practice tools
10. About/trust
11. FAQ
12. Lower disabled manual slot

## Problems

The same destinations recur across several adjacent groups. The homepage asks visitors to interpret content formats before establishing three simple goals. Quotes receive two substantial sections. The first-time and returning-user paths are both present but compete with catalogue breadth.

## Proposed section order

1. Hero: what Echo Buddha is, one primary beginner action, two restrained returning/practice actions
2. Three core pathways: Learn, Practice, Read & Reflect
3. Beginner path paired with today's reflection
4. Selected recent editorial reading
5. One combined returning-practice panel for Daily Reflection, Quotes, and Tools
6. Restrained publisher/trust cue
7. Existing FAQ

## Primary first-time journey

Homepage → Start Here → Buddhism for Beginners → first foundation. Start Here is the sole primary hero button.

## Primary returning-user journey

Homepage → Today's Reflection directly, with Meditation available as the second restrained action. Quotes and Tools remain one section lower.

## Sections removed or merged

- Merge five need paths and six guide cards into three distinct goal pathways.
- Remove the duplicate resource-link row and repeated “New here?” callout.
- Remove the standalone featured-quote section and eight-card quote grid.
- Merge quote/tool recurrence into one compact return panel.
- Reduce recent article cards from six to three to avoid archive behavior.
- Keep trust and FAQ because they answer first-time verification and beginner questions without dominating.

## Cards reduced/added

Pathway choices reduce from eleven repeated cards to three goal cards. Article cards reduce from six to three. Quote cards reduce from nine homepage cards to one compact direct link. No new content page or URL is added.

## Trust impact

The existing publisher explanation remains and gains direct Sources and Content Process links. No identity, credential, team, reviewer, or source claim changes.

## Mobile impact

Fewer first-screen and early-page choices, shorter card stacks, and no new interaction or hydration. The hero retains one primary and two secondary links that wrap naturally.

## Protected content

SEO title/description/canonical, AdSense verification/script behavior, disabled manual slots, FAQ truth, publisher language, beginner-owner links, meditation safety paths, indexability, and URLs remain unchanged.

## Rollback

Restore the prior homepage arrays/sections and CSS from the Phase 7 starting commit. No data migration or route rollback is needed.
