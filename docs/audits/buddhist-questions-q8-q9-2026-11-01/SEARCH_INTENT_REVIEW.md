# Q8 and Q9 search-intent review

Date: 2026-11-01

## Decision

Both routes have distinct reader intent and independent value. They extend the bounded Questions About Buddhism series from seven to nine detail pages. No alias, Q10 placeholder, “100 questions” hub, or alternate canonical is authorized.

## Q8 owner

`/learn/questions-about-buddhism/did-buddhists-agree-on-image-veneration/`

Primary need: assess the historical-consensus claim built from the Kathāvatthu’s silence about Buddha-image veneration.

Q8 does not absorb:

- Q1’s command/prohibition question;
- Q6’s `appaṭimo` and representation question;
- Q7’s first-image chronology question.

Its unique value is the argument-from-silence analysis, direct Kathāvatthu term/index search, chronology boundary, and contrast with later normalized image practice.

## Q9 owner

`/learn/questions-about-buddhism/which-cetiya-gives-more-merit/`

Primary need: distinguish traditional ceremonial precedence from a claim that one object category mechanically produces greater karmic fruit.

Q9 does not absorb:

- Q2’s wider account of post-Parinibbāna respect;
- Q3’s definition and scope of Uddesika Cetiya;
- Q5’s explanation of the Bodhi tree’s memorial role.

Its unique value is the separation of ceremonial order, direct physical connection, and individual merit, with bounded use of AN 6.63, AN 6.37, and Sri Lankan practice evidence.

## Cannibalization and discoverability

The hub owns collection navigation; each detail page owns one answer. The homepage remains curated to Q2 as its sole representative gateway. Q8 and Q9 appear once in the sitemap and once in internal search, use self-canonicals, and link to existing owners instead of repeating their full explanations.

## Indexing and monetization

Both routes are `index, follow`, substantive, self-canonical Learn detail pages. Both remain `LEARN_DETAIL / HOLD_MANUAL_REVIEW`. This release does not enable AdSense runtime, add ad placements, or submit an AdSense review.
