# Research and source decisions — checked October 23, 2026

- [Google: AI features and websites](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features): standard technical/content eligibility, visible useful text, internal links, matching schema; no special AI file or GEO schema requirement. No indexing or AI citation guarantee.
- [Google: Article structured data](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/article) and [publication dates](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/publication-dates): use accurate dates and identities matching visible content. A missing legacy publication date is preferable to an invented one.
- [Cloudflare HTML handling](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/static-assets/routing/advanced/html-handling/): built-in HTML normalization uses 307, including force-trailing-slash. Merely changing html_handling would not deliver permanent redirects. Exact generated asset redirect rules provide the supported, smaller solution without adding a Worker execution path.
- [IndexNow protocol](https://www.indexnow.org/documentation): changed URLs and ownership proof, with accepted receipt distinct from indexing. Keep the existing implementation.
- [Google Analytics pageviews](https://developers.google.com/analytics/devguides/collection/ga4/views) and [enhanced measurement](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/9216061): default page_location includes the URL; site search collects raw search_term; history-based pageviews operate separately. Configure safe page metadata, disable those automatic search/history behaviors, and count search use without text.
- [SN 56.11](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/sn/sn56/sn56.011.than.html), [SN 45.8](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/sn/sn45/sn45.008.than.html), [MN 44](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/mn/mn.044.than.html): four truth/tasks, path definitions, and threefold grouping. Original comparisons and practical examples are labeled as paraphrase/application, never copied translation.
- [MN 58](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/mn/mn.058.than.html), [MN 118](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/mn/mn.118.than.html), [AN 3.65](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/an/an03/an03.065.than.html): careful speech, full breathing-training scope, and inquiry that includes consequences and the wise rather than personal preference alone.
- [Dhammapada 184](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/kn/dhp/dhp.14.budd.html), [277–279](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/kn/dhp/dhp.20.budd.html), [Sn 1.8 / Khp 9](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/kn/snp/snp.1.08.than.html), [Five Precepts](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/ptf/dhamma/sila/pancasila.html): accurate source identity and scope. No modern staged meditation script is attributed verbatim to a sutta.

## Targeted comparison, not a ranking/backlink audit

Representative current resources inspected/discovered: [Tricycle beginners](https://tricycle.org/beginners/), [Lion's Roar meditation guide](https://www.lionsroar.com/how-to-meditate/), [Access to Insight source library](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/), [Declutter the Mind meditation help](https://help.declutterthemind.com/meditation), and [Jack Kornfield beginner resource](https://jackkornfield.com/meditation-for-beginners/).

The useful comparisons are a clear first-learning route, concrete practice questions, explicit source context, and identifiable editorial boundaries. They do not establish competitors' rankings, backlink strength, or measured performance. Echo Buddha's opportunity is to connect an accessible practice with a verifiable source while distinguishing early texts, later interpretation, modern application, and safety. The existing handbook and historical-question series already serve this purpose and were preserved. No competing wording or proprietary layout was copied; no competitor Lighthouse or backlink measurement is claimed.
