# Post-Approval Activation Checklist

This checklist is not authorization to activate. Complete in a new, owner-approved phase.

- [ ] Confirm AdSense site approval and exact publisher account.
- [ ] Confirm Auto Ads and all overlay formats remain off.
- [ ] Complete Google-certified CMP and legal/privacy review.
- [ ] Revalidate all 340 inventory rows and current official policies.
- [ ] Re-review every candidate and proposed zone on mobile and desktop.
- [ ] Add one manual test slot through the central component; never raw template code.
- [ ] Enable approval, runtime, serving and manual-slot gates through a reviewed change.
- [ ] Verify excluded routes and protected zones produce zero requests.
- [ ] Verify consent order, accessibility, CLS, density and accidental-click safeguards.
- [ ] Record exact release SHA, rollback target, account changes and network evidence.
- [ ] Activate gradually; monitor policy, UX and invalid-traffic signals.
