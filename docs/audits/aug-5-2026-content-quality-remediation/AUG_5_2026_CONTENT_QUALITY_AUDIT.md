## Executive Pre-Edit Verdict

The 19 August 5 pages are broadly useful and aligned with Echo Buddha's calm Buddhist-learning purpose, but the initial audit found several evidence-backed improvements worth implementing before long-term production confidence:

- Add direct source support for language/history claims on Dhamma/Dharma, Sutta, and Pali Canon pages.
- Add institutional support and stronger local-variation language for Buddhist temple etiquette.
- Tighten source/translation distinction for Dhammapada Verse 1 and sutta-study pages.
- Make Right Speech examples more explicitly anchored in the traditional four abstentions while keeping modern examples labeled as editorial applications.
- Strengthen meditation and relationship safety language without turning the pages into clinical, legal, or generic self-help advice.
- Reduce template feel by making several pages more scenario-specific rather than repeating the same "small practice" rhythm.

The audit does not recommend URL changes, redirects, merges, deletions, noindex changes, or new pages. The August 6 AdSense, consent, privacy, crawler, and route-gating work must remain untouched.
