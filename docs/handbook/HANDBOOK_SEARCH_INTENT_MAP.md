## Discovery and cannibalization controls

- Existing URLs, title ownership, and canonical ownership were preserved; no redirects or replacement routes were introduced.
- Each route has one self-canonical search-index record and one sitemap URL.
- Search metadata includes legitimate variants such as `Saṅgha`/`Sangha`, `Viṣṇu`/`Vishnu`, and `Theravāda`/`Theravada` without invisible keyword blocks.
- The handbook supplies depth; beginner lessons retain beginner intent; Deeper Questions retain source-critical historical intent.
- Hub → chapter, chapter → hub, previous/next, and three contextual related-learning links remain crawlable `<a href>` links.
