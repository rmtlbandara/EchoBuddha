## Discovery requirements

- Each route has one self-canonical search-index record and one sitemap URL.
- Hub aliases include both diacritic and non-diacritic spellings: `Saṅgha` and `Sangha`.
- Required discoverability terms are present across the collection: Three Jewels, Triple Gem, refuge, taking refuge, Buddha refuge, Dhamma refuge, Saṅgha/Sangha, devas, stupa, cetiya, Bodhi tree, lay Buddhist, bhikkhu, and monastic community.
- Home and Learn link to the hub. Every chapter links back to the hub, to its sequence neighbors, and to three relevant existing pages.
